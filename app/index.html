WSE Staff Portal

Add the portal to your phone or computer as an app.

On iPhone or iPad (Safari)
  1. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. The WSE icon appears on your home screen.
On iPhone or iPad
  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
On Android (Chrome)
  1. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
  3. Tap Install.
On a computer (Chrome or Edge)
  1. Click the install icon at the right of the address bar, or open the browser menu.
  2. Choose Install WSE Staff Portal (Edge: Apps → Install this site as an app).
Installed ✓Open WSE Staff Portal from your home screen or apps list. Sign in with your usual account.
